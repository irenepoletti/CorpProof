# CorpProof: Automated Sustainability Disclosure Platform 🌿📊

> **B2B SaaS RegTech Product Case Study**  
> Streamlining EU CSRD (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive) and ESRS compliance through automated evidence mapping, rigorous data auditing, and transparent AI governance under Art. 50 of the EU AI Act.

---

## 🚀 Executive Summary
**CorpProof** is an enterprise-grade RegTech platform designed to solve the critical friction in corporate sustainability reporting: transforming scattered enterprise data into auditable, regulatory-compliant ESRS disclosures. By combining audit-grade rigor in risk verification with intelligent workflow automation, CorpProof reduces compliance overhead while ensuring complete traceability.

---

## ⚙️ Core Product Modules

1. **Evidence Mapping Engine:** Automatically ingests financial, operational, and HR data pipelines to map enterprise records directly to specific ESRS data points.
2. **ESRS Requirement Checker:** Real-time gap analysis and materiality assessment module ensuring completeness before external audit.
3. **AI Review Trail (EU AI Act Compliant):** Governance layer operating under Article 50, providing human-in-the-loop oversight, immutable logs, and explainable AI audit trails for every automated disclosure suggestion.

---

## 🗺️ Product Roadmap & Expansion Strategy
- **Phase 1 (MVP & Validation):** Core evidence mapping and rule-based ESRS validation engine.
- **Phase 2 (AI Governance & Scaling):** Integration of the Article 50 compliance review trail and automated audit workflows.
- **Phase 3 (Go-To-Market):** Strategic regional expansion targeting mid-market and enterprise segments in **The Netherlands** and **Germany**.
