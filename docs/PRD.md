# Product Requirements Document (PRD): CorpProof

## 1. Problem Statement
Enterprise reporting under the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) requires exhaustive, auditable data collection across Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions alongside rigorous social and governance metrics. Manual collection is error-prone, costly, and lacks standardized audit trails.

## 2. Target Persona
- **Chief Sustainability Officer (CSO) / ESG Director:** Needs guaranteed compliance and clear visibility into reporting gaps.
- **Corporate Auditors & Compliance Officers:** Require immutable audit trails, evidence lineage, and verification speed.

## 3. Key Functional Requirements (FRs)
- **FR-01 Data Ingestion:** Secure connectors for ERP, HR systems, and automated Excel/CSV pipelines.
- **FR-02 Automated Mapping:** Rule-based matching engine mapping raw metrics to ESRS datapoints.
- **FR-03 Human-in-the-Loop Review:** Mandatory human sign-off workflow for all AI-assisted data classifications.
